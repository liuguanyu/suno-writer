# 朋克制作指南

在用户要求 Punk、朋克、Punk Rock、Pop Punk、Hardcore、Skate Punk 或 Post-Hardcore 时读取，同时遵守 `../suno-tags.md`。后朋读取 `post-punk.md`，金属读取 `metal.md`。

> 证据边界：朋克子流派的 prompt 组合是社区经验，Suno 官方 glossary 未定义专属语法。速度、音色是方向提示。

## 核心特征

朋克强调原始能量、快速节奏、短而直接的歌曲结构、失真强力和弦、合声/喊叫式副歌和 DIY 粗粝感。

## Style 模板

```text
punk rock, raw energy, fast tempo, aggressive distorted power chords,
short direct song structure, urgent shouted vocal, gang-vocal chorus, DIY grit
```

可替换方向：

- Pop Punk：`pop punk, catchy fast hooks, punchy drums, youthful melodic vocal, palm-muted guitars`
- Hardcore：`hardcore punk, blistering tempo, aggressive shouted vocals, breakdowns, raw intensity`
- Skate Punk：`skate punk, driving fast rhythm, melodic lead over power chords, energetic gang vocals`
- Post-Hardcore：`post-hardcore, angular riffs, dynamic quiet-loud shifts, emotional screamed and sung vocals`

## 段落模板

```text
[Intro: fast distorted power chords, driving drums]
[Verse 1: urgent vocal, palm-muted guitar, tight rhythm]
[Chorus: shout-along hook, gang-vocal stack, full energy]
[Verse 2: same drive, slight variation, backing shouts]
[Bridge: breakdown or half-time section, tension]
[Final Chorus: fastest, loudest, abrupt ending]
```

朋克通常段落短、速度快，避免长叙事和过多细节标签。

## 人声与歌词

- 歌词短促、直接、重音清晰，副歌适合喊唱。
- `gang vocals`、`shouted backing vocals` 是概率性 cue。
- 中文朋克注意每行长度和停顿，避免吞字。

## 常见失败与降级

- **太像流行摇滚**：加强 `raw energy`、`aggressive`、`distorted power chords`，减少 `polished production`。
- **失去速度感**：写 `fast tempo`、`driving rhythm`，缩短段落。
- **能量散掉**：精简歌词，保留 shout-along hook。
- **变成金属**：弱化 `double-kick`、`low-tuned guitars`，回到朋克短促直接的表达。

## 资料与可信度

- 社区教程（HookGenius、WaveCanvas、Undetectr）：`distorted power chords, fast tempo, gang-vocal stacks` 等是经验性写法。
