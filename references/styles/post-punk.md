# 后朋制作指南

在用户要求 Post-Punk、后朋、哥特摇滚、Darkwave、Coldwave 或阴郁另类摇滚时读取，同时遵守 `../suno-tags.md`。朋克读取 `punk.md`，Synthpop/New Wave 偏合成器时读取 `synthpop.md`。

> 证据边界：Post-Punk 子流派 prompt 组合是社区经验，Suno 官方 glossary 未定义专属语法。

## 核心特征

后朋强调棱角分明的吉他、冷波低音线、情绪化抽离的人声、催眠式节奏和阴郁空间感，比朋克更克制、更氛围化，比 Synthwave 更吉他驱动。

## Style 模板

```text
post-punk, angular jagged guitars, coldwave bassline, moody detached vocal,
hypnotic rhythmic pulse, dark atmospheric reverb, 140 BPM feel
```

可替换方向：

- 哥特摇滚：`gothic rock, dark atmospheric guitars, dramatic baritone vocal, echoing drums`
- Darkwave：`darkwave, brooding synth pads, cold bass, haunting distant vocal`
- Coldwave：`coldwave, minimal stark rhythm, icy synths, detached vocal`
- New Wave 偏吉他：`post-punk new wave, angular guitar, danceable rhythm, aloof vocal`

## 段落模板

```text
[Intro: jagged guitar motif, cold bassline, sparse drums]
[Verse 1: detached vocal over hypnotic rhythm]
[Chorus: tense hook, angular guitar stabs, wider reverb]
[Verse 2: rhythmic variation, backing textures]
[Bridge: breakdown, bass and drums, spacious]
[Outro: motif decays into dark reverb]
```

## 人声与歌词

- 人声通常克制、抽离、情绪化，避免过于明亮或甜美。
- 可用 `spoken detached vocal` 或 `dramatic baritone` 表达阴郁感。
- 无字时读取 `wordless-vocals.md`，以低吟或哼唱作为氛围层。

## 常见失败与降级

- **太像普通摇滚**：加强 `angular guitars`、`coldwave bassline`、`hypnotic rhythm`。
- **变得太明亮**：删除 `bright`、`catchy pop hook`，强调 `dark`、`moody`、`detached`。
- **失去节奏律动**：写 `hypnotic rhythmic pulse`、`danceable tension`。
- **太像 Synthpop**：弱化明亮合成器，强调 `jagged guitars` 与 `cold bass`。

## 资料与可信度

- 社区教程与 AI 概览：`angular guitars, cold-wave basslines, moody, detached vocals, hypnotic rhythms` 是经验性写法。
