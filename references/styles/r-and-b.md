# R&B 与 Neo-Soul 制作指南

在用户要求 R&B、Soul、Neo-Soul、Contemporary R&B、丝滑律动或旋律型 Hip-Hop/R&B 时读取，同时遵守 `../suno-tags.md`。

## Style 设计

优先明确时代、groove、人声与和声颜色：

```text
contemporary Chinese R&B, slow pocket groove, warm Rhodes chords,
round sub bass, restrained syncopated drums, intimate soulful lead vocal,
soft stacked harmonies, spacious modern production
```

可替换方向：

- 经典灵魂：`classic soul, live rhythm section, warm organ, expressive lead`
- Neo-Soul：`neo-soul, laid-back pocket, Rhodes, muted guitar, jazz-influenced harmony`
- 现代 R&B：`contemporary R&B, minimal drums, deep sub bass, airy pads, close-mic vocal`
- R&B/Trap：`melodic R&B over sparse trap percussion, restrained 808, sung hook`

避免把 `classic soul live band`、`minimal futuristic R&B`、`aggressive drill` 同时设为主方向。

## 段落控制

```text
[Verse 1: intimate close-mic vocal, sparse Rhodes and bass]
[Pre-Chorus: harmony opens, drums pull back, tension rising]
[Chorus: memorable sung hook, soft stacked backing harmonies]
[Bridge: stripped-down vocal and Rhodes, brief ad-lib responses]
[Final Chorus: wider harmony stack, stronger lead, controlled vocal runs]
```

R&B 的可控核心通常是 groove、主唱 phrasing 和副歌和声，不是每一句都做转音。实际转音规则读取 `vocal-techniques.md`。

## 歌词与旋律提示

- 句子长度适中，给切分和拖拍留空间。
- 副歌使用较短、可重复的旋律句；主歌允许更接近口语。
- 要求旋律型说唱时写 `rhythmic sung phrasing` 或 `melodic rap verses, sung chorus`，避免只写含糊的 `rap R&B`。
- Ad-lib 直接写短回应，避免长句和主唱完全重叠。

## 常见失败与降级

- **变成普通 Pop**：加强 `laid-back pocket`、`Rhodes`、`syncopated phrasing`，减少泛化的 `catchy pop anthem`。
- **转音过多、咬字不清**：改为 `restrained vocal runs, clear diction`，只在 Bridge 或 Final Chorus 标一次。
- **和声遮住主唱**：写 `lead remains dominant, backing harmonies low in mix`。
- **鼓和 808 太重**：删除 `trap`，改为 `soft syncopated drums, round bass`。
