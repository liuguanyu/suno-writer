# Gospel 与福音合唱制作指南

在用户要求 Gospel、福音音乐、教堂合唱、赞美诗、灵歌或强烈 call-and-response 合唱时读取，同时遵守 `../suno-tags.md`。

## Style 方向

```text
contemporary gospel, soulful lead vocal, mixed choir call and response,
warm Hammond organ, piano, live bass and drums, handclaps,
rising dynamics, joyful church-hall ambience
```

可区分：

- Traditional Gospel：`traditional gospel choir, organ and piano, handclaps, communal response`
- Contemporary Gospel：`modern gospel, polished rhythm section, soulful lead, layered choir`
- Gospel Ballad：`slow gospel ballad, piano and organ, restrained drums, emotional choir swell`
- Gospel A cappella：同时读取 `acapella.md`，删除所有器乐正向词。

涉及宗教文本时尊重用户指定的信仰语境；不要擅自替换神名、教义或礼仪用语。

## 声部组织

Call and Response 通常比全程 SATB 复调更稳定：

```text
[Verse 1: soulful solo lead, choir hums softly]
[Pre-Chorus: choir answers each phrase with short responses]
[Lead vocal] 我仍相信光会来到
[Choir] 光会来到！

[Chorus: full choir, homophonic four-part harmony, clear melody on top]
[Bridge: preacher-like spoken lead, sparse organ, choir responses]
[Final Chorus: wider choir harmony, handclaps, rising ad-libs, decisive cadence]
```

若要 SATB，优先 `homophonic four-part harmony`，不要默认要求四条完整独立旋律。复杂复调规则读取 `../suno-tags.md`。

## 发展弧线

1. 独唱或钢琴/风琴开场。
2. 短回应进入。
3. 副歌形成齐唱或同节奏四部和声。
4. Bridge 做 spoken/preacher-like 对比。
5. Final Chorus 扩大和声、拍手与 ad-lib，但保持主旋律可辨。

## 常见失败与降级

- **合唱不明显**：缩短回应歌词，逐行写 `[Choir]`，先做 call-and-response。
- **变成泛化 Pop Choir**：加强 `gospel`, `Hammond organ`, `handclaps`, `church-hall ambience`，但避免堆叠刻板化词汇。
- **SATB 混乱**：降为 `mixed choir, rich homophonic harmony, clear lead melody`。
- **Ad-lib 遮住歌词**：只在句间和终副歌使用 `sparse ad-libs`。
